# Viral Thumbnail Pro Skill

## 1. The Anatomy of a 1,000+ View/Hour Cover
For short-form vertical videos (YouTube Shorts & Instagram Reels), your cover thumbnail is what turns impressions into clicks when appearing in:
- The YouTube Shorts Grid / Search Results / Channel Page
- The Instagram Explore Grid / Profile Reels Tab

### The 4 Core Commandments:
1. **The Human Reaction Element (Emotion Anchor):**
   - 70% of clicks are driven by exaggerated human facial expressions (mouth open, scream, laugh, terror, confusion).
   - The thumbnail engine extracts keyframes at the highest audio decibel spike.
2. **The 3-Word Rule (Punchy Hook):**
   - Never write a full sentence on a thumbnail. Maximum 2 to 4 words.
   - Examples: `HE LOST IT 💀`, `BANNED LIVE 🚨`, `UNREAL GLITCH 🔥`, `NO WAY 😭`, `POLICE HEIST 🚔`.
3. **High-Contrast Palette (OLED Pop):**
   - Background: Desaturated or darkened gaming background with slight blur.
   - Text: High-visibility bright yellow (`#FFEE00`) or white (`#FFFFFF`) with a thick black stroke (`stroke_width=6-8px`) and red background badge.
4. **Visual Direction (Arrows / Circles):**
   - Subtle red or neon arrows pointing toward the critical in-game event (e.g. police car, hidden easter egg).

---

## 2. Technical Rendering Specs
- **Aspect Ratio:** `9:16` (1080 x 1920 px).
- **Safe Zone:** Keep key faces and text within the middle 60% vertical box (between Y=250 and Y=1450) so platform UI overlays (like buttons, channel handle, caption text) never obscure the thumbnail.
- **Color Grading:** +25% saturation, +20% contrast, subtle vignette on top and bottom borders.
