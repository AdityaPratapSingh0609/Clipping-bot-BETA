# The Definitive Algorithmic Video Training Bible

## 1. The Physics of the 0–1.5 Second Hook
The YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels recommendation engines evaluate one primary survival metric in the first 2 hours: **Swipe-Away Percentage**.
- If > 30% of users swipe away in the first 2 seconds, distribution is throttled.
- If < 15% swipe away, the video is pushed into wide-scale feed expansion.

### The 4 Mandatory Rules of the 0-Second Frame:
1. **Zero Cold Open Fluff**: Never begin with a greeting, intro screen, or silence. The clip must start on the shout, collision, gasp, or punchline.
2. **The Visual Pattern Interrupt**: A high-contrast on-screen text badge placed in the upper-middle third (safe zone):
   - `DO NOT BLINK 💀`
   - `BRO THOUGHT HE WAS SAFE 🚨`
   - `THIS ENDED HIS CAREER 😭`
3. **Audio Pre-Roll / J-Cut**: Audio hits 0.1s before visual action (e.g. scream, tire screech, vine boom).
4. **Zoom Punch-In**: Key reaction moments must punch in by 1.05x to 1.15x to reset viewer eye fixation.

## 2. Infinite Loop Architecture (Target APV: 110% – 140%)
Average Percentage Viewed (APV) dictates algorithmic breakout. To exceed 100% completion:
1. **The Circular Sentence**:
   - The final spoken sentence of the video must flow grammatically into the first spoken sentence.
   - Example:
     - End of video: *"And that's why..."*
     - Start of video: *"Nobody can escape the police in GTA 6!"*
2. **Identical Visual Framing**:
   - The final 5 frames and the opening 5 frames share the same camera crop or character position, creating a seamless re-watch.

## 3. Sends-Per-Reach (SPR): The #1 Viral Multiplier on Instagram
According to Meta’s engineering disclosures, **Direct Message (DM) Shares** carry **5x more recommendation weight** than likes or comments.

### The 4 High-SPR Content Archetypes:
| Archetype | Human Reaction | Caption Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| **The Incompetent Duo** | "Bro this is literally you" | *"Send this to your friend who drives like this in GTA 💀"* |
| **The Unbelievable Glitch** | "Did you see this?!" | *"Tag someone who needs to see this before Rockstar patches it 😭"* |
| **The Second-Hand Embarrassment** | "I'm dying laughing" | *"I couldn't even finish watching this without crying 😂"* |
| **The High-Stakes Bet** | "No way he pulled that off" | *"Who owes you $100 if they fail this heist? 👇"* |
