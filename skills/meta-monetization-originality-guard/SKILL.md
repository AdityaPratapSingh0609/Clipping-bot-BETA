# Meta & YouTube Monetization Originality Guard

## 1. Why Clipping Channels Get Flagged for "Unoriginal Content"
Meta (Instagram/Facebook) and YouTube use automated perceptual hashing (Audio Fingerprinting + Computer Vision Hash):
- **Raw Stream Cuts**: Uploading an unmodified section of another creator's stream triggers automated Content ID and Rights Manager matches.
- **Lack of Meaningful Transformation**: If the video has no distinct editorial framing, commentary, voiceover, sound design, or visual modifications, Meta classifies it as *"Aggregated or Repurposed Content with Limited Added Value"*, which removes monetization.

---

## 2. The 5-Point Transformative Editing Framework (100% Safe)

To pass monetization reviews and protect your account from originality penalties, every clip must undergo **Transformative Alteration**:

### A. Visual Hash Transformation (Bypasses Frame Hashing)
1. **Dynamic Dynamic Scale / Punch-In**:
   - Scale video slightly (`1.03x` to `1.05x`) with a slight crop. This alters the pixel coordinates and bypasses exact hash matches.
2. **Color Grading & Gamma Shift**:
   - Apply subtle saturation (`+15%`), contrast (`+10%`), and subtle warm color grading (`+2%` red/yellow channel).
3. **Animated Subtitles & Kinetic Typography**:
   - Burn-in dynamic word-by-word animated subtitles with custom color highlights (e.g. yellow on key phrases). Because subtitles change on every single frame, the frame hash becomes completely unique.
4. **Branded Visual Watermark / Attribution Badge**:
   - Add a subtle `@mild.lywashed` lower-third or top-corner badge throughout the video.

### B. Audio Frequency & Pitch Modulation (Bypasses Audio Fingerprinting)
1. **Micro-Pitch Shift**:
   - Apply a `+0.7%` to `+1.2%` pitch adjustment (or `1.02x` tempo adjustment). The human ear cannot distinguish the difference, but automated acoustic fingerprint algorithms fail to match it verbatim against the broadcast source.
2. **Dynamic Sound Effects (SFX Layer)**:
   - Layer whooshes, subtle risers, or punch impacts at key reaction moments. This creates an original multi-track audio mix that does not match the streamer's raw stream file.

### C. Editorial & Curatorial Value (Fair Use Defense)
1. **Transformative Title & Context**:
   - Instead of raw title *"Carry plays GTA"*, use editorial framing: *"Context: CarryMinati attempts the hardest GTA 6 escape but this glitch ruined everything..."*
2. **Fair Use Disclaimer in Caption**:
   > *"Curated & edited with transformative commentary and sound design by @mild.lywashed. Original broadcast featuring [Creator]. Under Fair Use / Creative Curation guidelines."*

## 3. Step-by-Step Monetization Recovery Protocol
If your Instagram account has already received the *"Content can't be monetized"* flag:
1. **Audit & Delete Raw Reposts**: Delete or archive any clips that were posted raw with zero captions or zero editing.
2. **Publish 10–15 Transformed Clips**: Use the PeakCut transformative pipeline (subtitles + zoom + color grade + branded badge + SFX).
3. **Post 3–5 Original Stories / Face Cam / Behind-The-Scenes**: Meta checks whether a real human operates the account. Posting 2–3 live selfie stories or setup videos proves human management.
4. **Submit for Review**:
   - Open Instagram App > **Professional Dashboard** > **Monetization** > **Policy Issues** > **Request Review**.
   - Review typically takes 3 to 7 business days.
